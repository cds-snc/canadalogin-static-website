GC Sign in for departments and agencies partners

A secure and easy way to access Government of Canada programs and services online

What is GC Sign in

  • Simplified sign-in

    Provide users with a simple and secure doorway to your service, that includes the latest security standards

  • Enhanced security through MFA

    Users can sign in using assurance Level 2 authentication, supported through SMS or voice verification

  • Continuous evolution

    GC Sign in will evolve over time (through CDS’s product management approach), as threats, user needs, and departmental needs evolve.

    GC Sign in is set to become the single sign in solution for all Government of Canada services Designed with users in mind, it offers a more intuitive, inclusive experience that makes accessing government programs easier than ever. For service providers, GC Sign in streamlines adoption and reduces overhead by eliminating the need for custom authentication systems. This centralized approach supports a more efficient, secure, and scalable digital ecosystem across departments. Want to see what’s next?     GC Sign in will help protect user accounts in several ways. Bulletted list below not showing. Look into it.
    • First, it will provide guidance to users on how to create more secure sign ins (e.g., strong passphrases, passkeys over passwords).
    • Second, GC Sign in (and the SaaS it's built on) will be continually updated to address the evolving security landscape.
    • Third, GC Sign in will eventually use telemetry and risk-based authentication to provide additional security.
     Finally, GC Sign in will have monitoring and notification abilities to detect and alert to suspicious activity.
