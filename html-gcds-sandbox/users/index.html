GC Sign in for individuals and organisations

Say goodbye to multiple accounts and passwords. GC Sign in is designed to simplify your experience, protect your information, and help you get to where you need to go faster.

What is GC Sign in

  • One login method

    With GC Sign in, you can access multiple government services and programs without having to create new usernames and passwords for each one.

  • Security and privacy

    GC Sign in uses best-in-class technology and security that meets Government of Canada standards. It also uses two-factor authentication to help better protect your identity.

  • Setting up your account

    Simply create an account using your email address and set up your password. Then, use that account to access participating Government of Canada programs and services.
