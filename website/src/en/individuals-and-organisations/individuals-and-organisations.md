# What is GC Sign in

<section>
  <gcds-grid tag="ul" columns-desktop="1fr 1fr 1fr" columns-tablet="1fr 1fr 1fr" columns="1fr">
    <li class="list-none">
      <img class="mb-150" src="../../images/common/home/icon-components.svg" alt="">
      <p><i class="fa-solid fa-people-group fa-4x"></i></p>
      <h3 class="mt-0">One login method</h3>
      <p>With GC Sign in, you can access multiple government services and programs without having to create new usernames and passwords for each one.</p>
    </li>
    <li class="list-none">
      <img class="mb-150" src="../../images/common/home/icon-components.svg" alt="">
      <p><i class="fa-brands fa-canadian-maple-leaf fa-4x"></i></p>
      <h3 class="mt-0">Security and privacy</h3>
      <p>GC Sign in uses best-in-class technology and security that meets Government of Canada standards. It also uses two-factor authentication to help better protect your identity.</p>     
    </li>
    <li class="list-none">
      <img class="mb-150" src="../../images/common/home/icon-components.svg" alt="">
      <p><i class="fa-brands fa-canadian-maple-leaf fa-4x"></i></p>
      <h3 class="mt-0">Setting up your account</h3>
      <p>Simply create an account using your email address and set up your password. Then, use that account to access participating Government of Canada programs and services.</p>     
    </li>
  </gcds-grid>
</section>
