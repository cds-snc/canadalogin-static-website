# About GC Sign in

## About the GC Sign in team

GC Sign in is developed by the **Canadian Digital Service (CDS)**. CDS is part of **[Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC)](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development.html)**. We aim to advance the goals of the [**Digital Ambition**](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/government-canada-digital-operations-strategic-plans/canada-digital-ambition.html) and improve service experiences in the Government of Canada. We build digital products that solve common service problems for departments.

Funded through [Budget 2024](https://www.budget.canada.ca/2024/home-accueil-en.html), GC Sign in was created to provide a single front door for federal government programs and services. 

[Learn more about CDS](https://digital.canada.ca/about/).
